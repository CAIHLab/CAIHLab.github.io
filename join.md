<section class="join-visible-section" aria-labelledby="collaborate-heading">
  <p class="eyebrow">Partnerships</p>
  <h2 id="collaborate-heading">Collaborate With Us</h2>
  <p>We welcome collaborations with clinicians, healthcare organisations, health service providers, industry and academic researchers. Potential collaborations include joint research projects, AI method development and evaluation, co-supervised PhD projects, student placements, joint funding applications and translation of research into real-world settings.</p>
  <p>If you have a problem where causal AI, agentic AI or personalised decision-making may be relevant, we would be interested in hearing from you.</p>
